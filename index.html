About Me

Planner. Researcher. City Maker.

A practitioner at the intersection of urban policy, spatial intelligence, and climate resilience.

An Urban Planner with 20 years of professional experience spanning urban policy, spatial planning, climate adaptation, and sustainable infrastructure across Bangladesh.

My work has taken me from field surveys in rural upazilas to coordinating flagship programmes with UN-Habitat, GIZ, and JICA. I believe good planning is both a technical discipline and a deeply human practice — built on listening, evidence, and the courage to challenge the status quo.

Currently, I work for UN ESCAP as a National Consultant in Sinking Cities in Asia-Pacific.
